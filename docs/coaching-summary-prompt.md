# Coaching summary prompt

Paste this into each client's Claude chat. Copy the reply into the **Coaching summary** box
on that client's page in the admin panel.

The protocol generator reads this instead of the intake form, so anything missing here is
missing from every protocol that client ever gets.

---

```
Read this entire conversation from the beginning, then write a factual profile of this
client for my coaching records.

Write it in plain text, third person, no markdown, no headings in bold, no bullet symbols
other than plain dashes. Use the section labels below exactly as written, each on its own
line, with the details underneath.

Rules:
- Only state what appears in this conversation. Never infer, never fill gaps, never guess.
- If something was never discussed, write "not discussed" under that label. Do not skip
  the label.
- Keep every number, measurement, dose, time, brand and name exactly as stated.
- Include their own words in quotes where the phrasing matters, especially for how they
  describe themselves and what they want.
- No advice, no recommendations, no protocol. This is a record of the man, not a plan.
- Contradictions get recorded as contradictions, with what changed and roughly when.
- Aim for 600 to 1200 words. Detail beats brevity.

BASICS
Age, location, time zone, occupation, work pattern, relationship and family situation,
who cooks, who else the routine has to fit around.

WHAT HE WANTS
The outcome in his words. Any numbers or deadlines. What is driving it. What he says his
life looks like if it works.

DAILY SCHEDULE
Wake time, work hours, meal times, training window, evening routine, bedtime. Weekdays and
weekends if they differ. Travel frequency and what the routine looks like while travelling.

NUTRITION
What he actually eats on a normal day, with timing. Cooking oils and eating out. Alcohol,
caffeine, nicotine, anything else, with amounts. Foods he refuses or reacts to. Budget or
access constraints.

TRAINING
Current split, frequency, session length, exercises, weights, how long he has trained,
injuries and physical limitations, what has stalled, what he enjoys or avoids.

SLEEP
Hours, quality, wake-ups, time to fall asleep, screens, what he has already tried.

HORMONAL AND ENERGY SIGNALS
Energy through the day and any crash times. Morning erections, libido, mood, recovery,
temperature, digestion, skin, hair. Anything he reported about how his body is behaving.

HEALTH HISTORY
Diagnoses, medications, TRT or peptides or any prescriptions, supplements with doses,
surgeries, ongoing conditions, family history. Any bloodwork with the actual numbers,
units and dates.

PSYCHOLOGY AND IDENTITY
How he handles conflict, criticism and authority. Where he shrinks and where he is solid.
How he sees himself. Validation sources. Confidence in professional and sexual settings.
Patterns in his relationships. What he avoids. What he is ashamed of if he said it.

WHAT HE HAS ALREADY TRIED
Previous coaches, diets, programmes and protocols. What worked, what failed, and his own
explanation of why.

WHAT I HAVE ALREADY GIVEN HIM
Everything I prescribed in this conversation. What he executed, what he ignored, what he
pushed back on. Where he is in the process right now.

HOW HE RESPONDS TO COACHING
Compliance, honesty, excuses, how he takes directness, how much hand-holding he needs,
how he communicates.

RED FLAGS AND CONSTRAINTS
Anything that changes what can safely or realistically be prescribed. Medical, financial,
time, family, psychological.

OPEN QUESTIONS
What I still do not know about this man that matters.
```

---

## After pasting

Once a summary is in, the client's page stops showing the amber "No coaching summary"
warning and the Generate button turns on. Generate the protocol and read it before
sending — the summary is the only thing standing between the client and a generic
document.
